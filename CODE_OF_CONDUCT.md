# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Keep discussion professional, technical, and respectful. Assume contributors are trying to improve the project, and focus criticism on code, behavior, risk, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information, repeated disruption, or pressuring maintainers to add spam, abuse, anti-detection, or restriction-bypass features.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this standard. Repeated or severe violations may result in blocked participation.
